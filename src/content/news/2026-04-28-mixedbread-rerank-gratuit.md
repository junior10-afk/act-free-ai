body_en: |-
  Mixedbread now offers a free tier on its embeddings and reranker, with no credit card.

  Two advantages for a project:

  - **The models are open**, so you can move from the hosted service to local execution as volume grows.
  - **The reranker is specialised** in semantic search, which complements an existing index well.

  The monthly quota covers a prototype and some load testing. Beyond that, compare API cost with a shared GPU: the switch pays off past a certain volume, which is worth calculating.
