body_en: |-
  Modal makes the monthly free compute credit clearer: current usage, projection and cost per deployed function.

  Why it helps:

  - **Hosting a small model** becomes predictable, with a clear ceiling.
  - **Comparing two approaches** (third-party API versus self-hosted model) becomes quantifiable.
  - **Cold start** shows up in the metrics, which helps sizing.

  The monthly credit will not cover a high-traffic service, especially with GPUs. It is an excellent sandbox for measuring, before deciding between paying a provider or provisioning a machine.
