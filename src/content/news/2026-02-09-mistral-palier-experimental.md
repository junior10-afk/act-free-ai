  - **Les limites sont par minute et par mois**, affichées dans la console. C'est plus simple à anticiper qu'un quota opaque.

  Mistral is adjusting its free tier: more models available, and rate limits documented explicitly.

  - **Usage remains experimental.** The terms forbid production on this tier; a public service needs billing enabled.
  - **The official SDK** handles retries, saving you from writing your own loop on 429 errors.

  A good test bench for comparing European models, provided you do not confuse prototype with production.
